      Privacy

      • Your questions go straight from this browser to the provider you chose. Renard has no server, and its developer never sees your questions, chats, or key.
      • With Read chat on, the conversation on the page is sent along with your question, only when you ask. It is never stored.
      • The site and the provider are independent. A ChatGPT conversation can be sent to Anthropic or Google, and a Claude conversation to OpenAI or Google.
      • On the Gemini API's free tier, Google's terms let it use what you send, including any chat Renard includes, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Requests on a paid Gemini plan are not used that way.
      • Your API key is stored on this device in the browser's extension storage, unencrypted, which is standard for bring-your-own-key extensions. Anyone with access to this browser profile could read it.
